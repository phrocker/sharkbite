    Learning console
    
          
    Datasets, distillation jobs, candidates and evaluations, releases, evidence, and offline release bundles, driven through the authenticated learning routes. Every action here has an equivalent route and produces the same record. Shadow evidence cannot promote a model or exclude review: promotion grants shadow-candidate status, and activation is an explicit, bound approval. This console creates no authority and cannot make an unmounted service appear available.

    Candidates and evaluations
    
            
    
            
    
              
              
            
    
            
    
              
    Seal a completed run: the host rebuilds the candidate, evaluation, and artifact from the manifest, receipt, prediction set, the policy declared before the receipt, and every export the run consumed, publishes the bundle on the serving root, and measures the student on the reserved split. The disposition is shadow_candidate or hold, never an authorization.
